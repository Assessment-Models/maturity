The 5 Levels of Maturity

Capabilities evolve through distinct stages. Knowing your current baseline eliminates guesswork when prioritizing your next investment.

Level 01

Initial / Ad-Hoc

Unpredictable, reactive workflows dependent on individual heroics rather than shared systems.

Level 02

Repeatable

Core practices work within isolated teams, though consistency across departments varies widely.

Level 03

Defined

Playbooks, tooling, and standards are documented and adopted as an organization-wide baseline.

Level 04

Managed

Performance is tracked quantitatively with real-time telemetry, enabling predictable delivery.

Level 05

Optimizing

Automated feedback loops and continuous experimentation fuel sustained industry leadership.
